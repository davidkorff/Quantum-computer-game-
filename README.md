# Quantum Quest 🔮⚛️

**The Complete Quantum Computing Educational Game**

Master quantum computing from theory to practice through an interactive, gamified learning experience. Built for aspiring quantum engineers and developers.

## 🎮 Play Now

**[Launch Quantum Quest](https://davidkorff.github.io/Quantum-computer-game-/)**

## 🌟 Features

### 📚 8 Learning Eras
- **Era 1: Quantum Mechanics Foundations** - Superposition, entanglement, measurement
- **Era 2: Birth of Quantum Computing** - Feynman's vision, Deutsch's algorithms
- **Era 3: Quantum Gates & Circuits** - Single-qubit, multi-qubit gates, universal sets
- **Era 4: Quantum Algorithms** - Grover's, Shor's, VQE, QAOA
- **Era 5: Quantum Hardware** - Superconducting, ion traps, photonics, neutral atoms
- **Era 6: Error Correction** - Decoherence, bit-flip, phase-flip, surface codes
- **Era 7: Quantum Programming** - Qiskit, Cirq, Q#, PennyLane
- **Era 8: Applications & Future** - Cryptography, optimization, simulation, QML

### 🔬 Interactive Quantum Lab
- **Visual Circuit Builder** - Drag-and-drop quantum gates
- **Real-time Simulation** - See state vectors and probabilities update instantly
- **Bloch Sphere Visualization** - Understand qubit states geometrically
- **Code Export** - Export circuits to Qiskit, Cirq, or Q#

### 💻 Coding Challenges
- **10+ Quantum Programming Challenges** - From beginner to expert
- **Built-in Simulator** - Run quantum code directly in the browser
- **Test Cases** - Automatic validation of your solutions
- **Hints System** - Get help when stuck

### 🏆 Gamification
- **XP & Leveling System** - Progress from "Quantum Curious" to "Quantum Legend"
- **30+ Achievements** - Unlock badges for milestones
- **Progress Tracking** - Your progress saves automatically
- **Progress Codes** - Transfer progress between devices (format: `QQ-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`)

## 🛠️ Technology

Built with pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript - no frameworks required!

- **Quantum Math Library** - Complex numbers, matrices, tensor products
- **Quantum Simulator** - State vector simulation up to 8 qubits
- **Canvas Rendering** - Hardware-accelerated circuit visualization
- **localStorage** - Client-side progress persistence
